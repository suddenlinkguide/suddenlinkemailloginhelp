Understanding IMAP and POP
==========================

IMAP
----

Advantages include:

* Synchronizes emails across multiple devices
* Keeps messages stored on the server
* Automatically updates folders
* Recommended for most users

POP
---

Benefits include:

* Downloads emails locally
* Supports offline access
* Suitable for users with one primary device

IMAP generally offers the best experience because it synchronizes your email across all devices.
